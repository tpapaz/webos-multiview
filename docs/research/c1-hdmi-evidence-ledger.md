# C1 HDMI research history and evidence ledger

LG OLED C1, firmware 03.53.45. [Trial readiness](../../STATUS.md#c1-trial-readiness) · [Device journal](../device-tests/c1-journal.md)

## Research history

| Stage | What we tried and learned | Why the path changed |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Local playback and window controls | Phase 0 verified passive inventory and exit. In Phase 1 two local clips advanced on separate main and secondary views. The IWC window-control path lacked verified restoration. [Phase 1 result](../device-tests/c1-journal.md#phase-1) · [IWC findings](c1-iwc-research.md) | The clips proved two presentation surfaces. We still needed to acquire physical HDMI in our own window. Idle work closed and the remaining Phase 1 checks were deferred. [Scope decision](../decisions/0003-close-idle-work-resume-hdmi.md) |
| 2. Native single HDMI | Direct UTP is the TV service path the native app used to open and select an input. We worked through registration, event-loop and exit problems. Version 0.1.12 showed eight seconds of moving HDMI 4 with sound and returned to normal viewing. [Development stages](../device-tests/c1-journal.md#first-picture-development-summary) · [HDMI 4 trial](../device-tests/c1-journal.md#0112-physical-hdmi-4--moving-picture-and-sound) | This gave us a working single-input control for the native pair design. |
| 3. Native HDMI 1 + HDMI 2 | We designed HDMI 1 on `MAIN` and HDMI 2 on the secondary display path, `SUB0`. Port selection creates and starts a fresh connector in one call. Stock HDMI 2 was observed on `MAIN`; the accepted ownership reply in that capture belonged to a different pipeline. G4 firmware supplied a comparison only. [Stock capture](../device-tests/c1-journal.md#stock-hdmi-2-switch--read-only-log-capture) · [G4 comparison](prior-art.md#retail-g4-comparison) | **Paused before a native two-input device trial.** We lacked an app-owned way to place HDMI 2 on the secondary path before its first scaler request and verify isolated cleanup. [Native design and gates](../device-tests/archive/c1-dual-hdmi-design.md#review-only-dual-source-experiment) |
| 4. Glasshouse-style JS preview | Glasshouse used an HTML video element with `service/webos-external` and `ext://hdmi:<port>`. The owner had seen it show a live HDMI 4 preview on this C1. Our standalone JS 0.1.0 probe also showed moving HDMI 4 with sound. [Glasshouse source and observation](c1-hdmi-interface-map.md#glasshouse-preview-source-and-owner-observation) · [Our preview](../device-tests/c1-journal.md#browser-hdmi-4--moving-picture-and-sound) | A second video element let us test whether the browser assigned separate paths by default. The available live sources were HDMI 4 and the laptop on HDMI 1. |
| 5. JS HDMI 4 + HDMI 1 | Pair 0.1.0 produced no visible first picture. In 0.1.1 the saved log showed the second press missed its deadline and HDMI 1 never started. With the longer window in 0.1.2, HDMI 1 played, HDMI 4 stopped, and HDMI 4 resumed when HDMI 1 closed. [First attempt](../device-tests/c1-journal.md#browser-hdmi-4-and-hdmi-1--exploratory-attempt) · [Recovered log](../device-tests/c1-journal.md#browser-hdmi-pair--ready-and-playback-retry) · [Completed pair trial](../device-tests/c1-journal.md#browser-hdmi-pair--40-second-retry) | Default routing produced sequential playback. The reviewed JS media options and compositor path exposed no verified per-input secondary selector. [JS sink selection](c1-hdmi-interface-map.md#can-javascript-select-the-hdmi-sink) · [Compositor findings](c1-hdmi-interface-map.md#can-the-compositor-or-multi-view-assign-sub0-before-playback) |
| 6. Native assistance for the JS session | We considered preparing a secondary session before JS playback, then moving HDMI 1 after it started. Probe 0.1.3 ran HDMI 1 alone. Of its two registered connectors, one requested `MAIN_SCALER`. Both proposed the backend as owner and both associations failed. [Helper design](c1-hdmi-interface-map.md#could-a-native-helper-prepare-hdmi-1-for-the-browser) · [HDMI 1 trace](../device-tests/c1-journal.md#browser-hdmi-1-connector-ownership) | We identified the video connector. An app-owned way to address it is still missing. The move and the subsequent HDMI 4 start were never attempted. This led us to check whether the stock HDMI caller already had a registered parent when opening the input. [Owner data flow](c1-hdmi-interface-map.md#where-does-the-hdmi-video-connector-get-its-owner) |
| 7. Stock HDMI caller ownership | Both stock InputCommon HDMI opens omit `options.mediaId`. A separate native caller sends the field and uses fixed port value 5. UTP conditionally forwards it on a fresh open. The recorded stock port-2 switch reached `MAIN`; the only accepted ownership reply belonged to a different pipeline. [Caller, firmware and capture evidence](c1-hdmi-interface-map.md#does-the-stock-hdmi-caller-supply-a-registered-parent) | **Helper move paused.** Reopen with evidence linking an already-registered parent, the physical HDMI open and that video child's accepted association. |
| 8. Starfish registration and fixed port 5 | On its empty-ID path, Starfish can register a media pipeline and pass the returned connection ID into its HDMI open. The subsequent switch selects port 5, a special playback route with hardware index 4. The configurable-port browser callers omit the parent ID. [Native caller trace](c1-hdmi-interface-map.md#which-native-caller-registers-the-parent-before-hdmi-open) · [Port mapping](c1-hdmi-interface-map.md#what-does-the-native-callers-fixed-port-5-select) | Closed Starfish as a selector for either ordinary jack. Physical wiring of the special input remains unknown. A distinct app-owned caller for physical HDMI 1 or 2 is still needed. |
| 9. Register a parent in our native helper | A pre-load Starfish media-ID getter compiled and linked offline. The ordinary UTP open clears even a supplied parent ID before the HDMI backend creates its session. [Helper feasibility](c1-hdmi-interface-map.md#would-our-native-helper-keep-its-registered-media-id-on-hdmi-1) | The playback frontend became a candidate because its video configuration still matches on port 1 and its fresh sink receives the retained parent ID. Physical playback and an accepted ownership reply for that child remain unverified. [Frontend trace and sound-path exception](c1-hdmi-interface-map.md#can-the-playback-frontend-route-physical-hdmi-1-and-preserve-the-owner) |
| 10. Physical HDMI 1 ownership API | The [registration helper](../build.md#native-media-registration-helper) was built locally. The saved C1 UTP binding, HDMI window setter, pin, property handler and stock sink wrappers serve other purposes. [Physical API findings](c1-hdmi-interface-map.md#can-a-physical-hdmi-1-api-attach-the-helpers-media-id) | Parked the helper trial pending a distinct physical-input caller or contract that can pass an app-owned parent to the video child. The helper has had no TV run. |
| 11. Diagnose where the pair handed off | The [pre-reconnect retry](../device-tests/c1-journal.md#hdmi-1-reconnect-under-observation) matched the HDMI 1 backend to a successful TVRM reply with no receiver entry. The [decoder](c1-hdmi-interface-map.md#offline-receiver-decoder) can identify an indexed media-server grant. The separate [stock HDMI 1 grant window](../device-tests/c1-journal.md#stock-hdmi-1-receiver-grant-window) closed cleanly with no reportable completion. | Closed this capture and returned to the HDMI 2 first-scaler question. Jack attribution from TVRM snapshots remains paused because the [logging review](c1-hdmi-interface-map.md#can-existing-c1-logs-establish-a-continuous-slot-lifetime) found no proof of continuous slot coverage. |
| 12. Recheck HDMI 2 before its first scaler request | The stock caller selects port 2, its guide PiP handler skips HDMI, and the ordinary video sink starts its own proxy with `MAIN`. The saved `SUB0` log entries lack child identities. The accepted owner in the combined capture belongs to a different pipeline. [Caller and receipt checks](c1-hdmi-interface-map.md#does-the-stock-hdmi-2-caller-select-sub0-before-its-first-scaler-request) | Paused pending a distinct physical caller or an observation that links the port-2 child to the applied type and first request. |
| 13. Resume the available HDMI 4 + laptop HDMI 1 pair | The ordinary browser pair handed off. Both ordinary HDMI 1 ownership captures rejected the video child’s proposed parent. A fresh playback-frontend open with a live app-registered parent is still untested. [Firmware and sound-path limits](c1-hdmi-interface-map.md#can-the-playback-frontend-route-physical-hdmi-1-and-preserve-the-owner) | The [parent-lifetime core](../build.md#hdmi-1-ownership-probe-core) passed host tests and linked against the ARM SDK. | 14. Assess HDMI 4 + a broadcast channel | The C1 browser tuner preview supplies a native window ID at open and sends stop and close with its broadcast ID. The saved preview path has no evidenced app-controlled secondary assignment. [Tuner trace](c1-hdmi-interface-map.md#built-in-tuner-preview) | Park the tuner trial and prepare HDMI 4 + a silent local clip, which has a tested secondary path in the local-only control. [Next test](../device-tests/current-plan.md#hdmi-4-and-a-silent-local-clip) |
| 15. Prepare HDMI 4 + a silent clip | Packaged 0.1.13 after injected worker stalls in startup, feed and unload. The C1 idle lifecycle passed. The playback preflight found zero coverage for the four HDMI methods and submitted no launch. [Build and checks](../build.md#hdmi-4-and-silent-clip-probe) · [C1 result](../device-tests/c1-journal.md#hdmi-4-and-silent-clip-0113-lifecycle) | Mixed playback stays open pending a separately scoped admission decision. ## Source combinations

The strongest C1 evidence is for two app-owned local video streams. Mixed
combinations depend on how the app gets each picture and which decoder,
presentation and foreground resources it needs.

| Combination | What the evidence supports |
|---|---|
| Local clip + local clip | **Verified** briefly on this C1 with explicit main and secondary presentation. Audio ownership remains unknown. [Local control](c1-smp-ndl-resource-path.md) |
| HDMI + local clip | **Candidate** with the strongest saved mixed-source evidence. A silent clip can target the native secondary route. Coexistence and cleanup under HDMI load remain unknown. [Timing check](c1-smp-ndl-resource-path.md#timing-against-the-hdmi-deadlines) |
| HDMI + network camera images | **Candidate.** MJPEG or snapshots can be drawn as images and avoid a second native video decoder. A homebrew project reports MJPEG overlay on G4. App-owned HDMI and the image overlay still need to be shown together on C1. [Camera overlay project](https://github.com/iDaddyCz/ha-lg-webos-pip) |
| Network stream + network stream | **Candidate.** Two decoded local clips give us a presentation path to try with streams the app can receive and decode. IPTV multiview implementations give a separate precedent. Bandwidth, startup, codec and decoder limits need measurement on the C1. [IPTV prior art](prior-art.md#network-playback-and-app-lifecycle-prior-art) |
| HDMI + IPTV or other network video | **Candidate.** Establish HDMI plus the controlled local clip first, then try an authorized network source on the same secondary playback route. Live startup, buffering and audio bring separate variables. |
| HDMI + built-in tuner | **Default browser overlap failed on the C1.** The channel took over the HDMI 4 picture and HDMI 4 resumed after tuner unload. A separately assigned tuner path remains a candidate. [Trial](../device-tests/c1-journal.md#hdmi-4-and-live-tuner-playback) |
| Built-in tuner + built-in tuner | **Playback untested on C1.** The reviewed C1 DVB routes cannot establish two allocations on the same tuner. [DVB attachment checks](c1-hdmi-interface-map.md#dvb-attachment-candidates) |
| HDMI + screen share or a separate TV app | **Unknown on C1.** LG documents specific pairs such as HDMI plus YouTube or screen share on supported models. A separate TV app also needs system support for foreground policy, focus and presentation, with its normal content protections in place. [LG Multi View guide](https://www.lg.com/us/support/help-library/lg-tv-how-to-use-multiview--20153280988637) |
| HDMI + HDMI | **Unknown for app-owned windows on C1.** LG documents two HDMI inputs in stock Multi View on specific models. A stock test there could establish supported formats and controller behavior on that firmware. [G4 specifications](https://www.lg.com/us/tvs/lg-oled77g4wua-oled-4k-tv) · [Saved G4 comparison](prior-art.md#retail-g4-comparison) |

Three simultaneous hardware video pictures remain **unknown**. Image overlays
can add views from compatible feeds while using a different rendering path.
