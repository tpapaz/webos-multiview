startup to app cleanup. The 0.1.11 setup-only trial returned from local
cleanup. Version 0.1.12 then passed a separate lifecycle trial with
independently observed termination. The physical trial showed moving
HDMI 4 with audible sound in the app's fixed window on C1 firmware
03.53.45. Normal viewing returned after exit.
